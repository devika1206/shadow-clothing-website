How do I get my files out of WhatsApp?
  1. Open the chat (e.g. Message yourself) in WhatsApp.
  2. Tap ⋮ → More → Export chat → Include media.
  3. Save the ZIP to your phone (or share it to Files / Drive).
  4. Open this page on the phone and choose that ZIP. Or choose the folder Android/media/com.whatsapp/WhatsApp/Media/WhatsApp Documents.
  5. Tick what you want, choose how to organise it, tap Download ZIP.
